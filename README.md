# Astris.ai

**Astris.ai** is an AI-powered past-paper revision platform that helps students practice exam papers online, annotate solutions, get instant AI grading, and receive real-time help on difficult questions.

## Features

* **Paper Management & Upload:** Upload, organise, and track your past examination papers in one central dashboard.
* **Built-in Canvas & Annotation Toolbar:** Write, draw, erase, and annotate directly on past papers using digital drawing tools (pen, highlighter, colour selectors, and stroke thickness controls).
* **Automated AI Marking:** Click **Mark Paper** to let the AI automatically evaluate your handwritten/annotated responses against official marking criteria and generate detailed score reports (e.g., total percentage scores).
* **Real-Time AI Study Assistance:** Embedded **AI Help** chat assistant to explain complex steps, break down concepts, or guide you through tough problems.
* **Daily Streak & Progress Analytics:** Stay consistent with daily streak tracking, activity calendars, target goals, and score average breakdowns.
* **Gamification & Social Features:** Built-in leaderboard and friends hub to keep study sessions engaging and competitive.
* **Dark Mode & Customisation:** Clean, distraction-free UI with theme support (Light/Dark/System).
